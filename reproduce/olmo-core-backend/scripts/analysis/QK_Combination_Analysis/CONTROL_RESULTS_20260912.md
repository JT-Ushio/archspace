## Interpretation for the next experiments

- Q/K show lower terminal training loss, but **not better final validation macro
  CE**. The statement “Q/K SiLU is better” must specify the metric and interval.
  The much larger last100 than last500 training gap also warrants reading the
  full curve rather than quoting only one terminal window.
- Q/K still outperform V/O on the last validation macro. No paired training
  seeds were run; neither significance nor equivalence is established.
- The largest Q/K source regression is dolma_stack: CE increases0.014334 forQ,
  0.017136 forK. Domain-specific effects deserve examination; do not select only
  sources on which the hypothesis succeeds.
- Keep the submitted QK combination as the missing2×2 cell. Its test is whether
  combining nonlinear Q/K yields a **validation** gain or interaction, not
  whether it further lowers training loss alone. Do not yet launch QK+V/O.
- Existing L/Q/K checkpoint analysis remains useful: same held-out samples,
  paired CE, layer/head attention and BF16/FP8 inference sensitivity. These can
  localize correlates but cannot prove a causal explanation or undo FP8 training.
